# 0.5 — Drink Catalogue & Apple Health

Draft for review. Based on `4d39ad03ad3bfc767eaf2c7cbcef64bddae0cc48`; no tag or distribution is approved by these notes. See the [release evidence](0.5-evidence.md) for verification and accepted limitations.

## What's included

- **60 drinks across ten categories**, with reviewed generic hydration, water and caffeine estimates, serving suggestions and supported preparation choices. Coffee offers shots and regular/decaf options where applicable; Alcohol offers selected strengths and supported non-alcoholic alternatives.
- **Catalogue-backed logging and preserved history.** Saved drinks retain their captured names, preparations and estimates when catalogue definitions change. Repeating an available recent drink creates a new entry using current rules.
- **Optional Apple Health export** for water, caffeine and alcoholic beverages. Each type has separate write permission and status. Water uses saved estimated water content, caffeine uses saved mass, and alcohol uses fractional 14 g standard drinks. Local logging works without Health permission.
- **Durable export and deletion handling.** Eligible new entries retain frozen quantities and dates for retries. One failed type does not resend successful types. Deleting a drink requests cleanup of its matching Watered-created samples, including while sync is off.
- **Add Drink and unit improvements.** Category-specific preparation space, a pinned Volume picker, selection haptics and consistent artwork presets. Profile units carry through drafts, Recents and saved-detail presentation without changing the physical logged amount.
- **Local-first foundations.** Persistence saves before success feedback; display-unit changes remain separate from goal history. Debug analytics stay local and Release discards events.

## Scope and known limitations

Estimates describe generic recipes, not measured personal hydration or medical safety thresholds. Alcoholic preparations receive zero hydration-goal credit while their full volume remains fluid intake.

Apple Health integration is export-only. Enabling it does not export existing history, drinks added while disabled or old entries for newly permitted types. Turning it off pauses uploads but leaves existing Health samples in place. Blocked deletion cleanup retains hidden local details until resolved. Retries do not guarantee background or exactly-once completion; an unacknowledged write may be recreated during recovery after manual deletion in Health.

Add Drink has known clipping at larger text sizes. Dynamic Type work and remaining spacing, transition and manual-expansion review are deferred to the detailed UI pass. Basic functionality was accepted for 0.5; comprehensive visual/accessibility verification is not claimed.

This release does not include Health import, iCloud sync, remote catalogue delivery, durable per-category remembered selections, nutrition/CBD tracking, milk overrides, historical recalculation, diagnostic warnings, widgets, a Watch app or production analytics collection.

Old enum-only development history is not automatically migrated or erased. Snapshot-backed history is preserved. See [Development Drink Data](../Development-Drink-Data.md); this is not a production migration guarantee.
