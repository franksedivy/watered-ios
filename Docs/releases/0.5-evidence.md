### Catalogue and export contract reconciliation

The bundle is content `development-25`, schema 6, manifest 1; snapshot calculation version 5. Counts are Water 4, Coffee 7, Tea 10, Juices 4, Soft Drinks 6, Wellness 3, Sports & Energy 4, Milk & Alternatives 12, Alcohol 8, Other 2. All 60 have reviewed generic water estimates: 52 fixed fractions and eight strength tables. Old missing estimates remain unknown.

The 64-entry proposal is reconciled in the catalogue reference: several coffee entries, standalone Fruit drink, Greens drink and Apple cider vinegar drink are omitted; Cola and supported non-alcoholic drinks use preparations; Electrolyte drink, Coconut milk, Ayran, Fortified wine and Liqueur are included. Serving suggestions are not mandatory amounts. Stable IDs, captured names/preparations, estimates and version context preserve historical meaning.

[Mapping](../decisions/001-healthkit-export-mapping.md) and [lifecycle](../decisions/002-healthkit-export-lifecycle.md) own the exact contract. Three worked examples, assuming eligible consent/permission:

| Saved drink | Water ml | Caffeine mg | Alcohol count |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 250 ml Latte, two regular shots | 225 | 120 | Omit |
| 330 ml Beer, 5% ABV | 306.9 | Omit: zero | `330 × 0.05 × 0.789 / 14` ≈ 0.929893 |
| 330 ml non-alcoholic Beer | 320.1 | Omit: zero | Omit: exact ABV unknown, upper bound 0.5% |

For saved `loggedAt = 2026-11-14T20:00:00Z`, start and end remain that instant on later retries and in other timezones. Eligibility is captured at new-entry creation using current app opt-in and per-type authorization, not inferred later from timestamps. Newly entered backdated drinks can qualify; old history, disabled gaps and previously unpermitted types do not backfill.

Retries preserve frozen quantity/unit/date, provenance, export policy and entry/type identity with sync version 1. Success for one type does not resend it when another fails. Local save and intents commit before success; Health errors do not undo local history. Deletion targets only matching Watered-owned entry/type samples. Hidden details and cleanup persist with sync off until resolved; no automatic expiry. Acknowledged samples deleted in Health are not recreated, but an unacknowledged successful write can be recreated during crash recovery. There is no Health import/read scope or iCloud implementation.
